Company Profile

Building Reliable Infrastructure Through Quality Manufacturing

Udhaya Spun Pipes is an RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) pipe manufacturing and wholesale supply company located at Moongiloorani, Kannar Street, Manamadurai, Sivaganga, Tamil Nadu. We manufacture and supply heavy-duty precast concrete spun pipes tailored for infrastructure contractors, civil engineering works, agricultural drainage, and municipal projects.

Our manufacturing operations focus specifically on NP2, NP3, and NP4 grade RCC pipes, encompassing multiple internal diameter options ranging from 150 mm up to 1400 mm. Designed to withstand subterranean pressures, earth overburden, and vehicular movement, our pipes deliver dependable structural integrity for roadways, culverts, and storm-water management.

With a flexible production capacity that can be maximised according to customer requirements, we cater efficiently to both scheduled wholesale procurement and urgent infrastructure timelines across Tamil Nadu.

Production Workflow

Our Manufacturing Approach

From selected raw materials to systematic curing and dispatch, our manufacturing workflow ensures consistent concrete density and dimensional reliability.

01

Raw Material Preparation

Careful sourcing and preparation of cement, graded coarse and fine aggregates, clean water, and high-tensile steel reinforcement cages according to required grade dimensions.

02

Mould Setup & Reinforcement

Precision mould preparation and placement of concentric steel reinforcement cages, ensuring exact wall thickness and dimensional consistency for centrifugal spinning.

03

Pipe Moulding & Compaction

Centrifugal spinning compaction using heavy gantry-supported moulds to expel air bubbles, delivering high-density concrete with impermeable walls and smooth internal bore.

04

Controlled Yard Curing

Controlled hydration and curing cycles in yard storage to cultivate high compressive strength, prevent shrinkage cracks, and prepare joint surfaces for field installation.

05

Dimensional & Quality Inspection

Systematic dimensional verification checking internal diameter (ID), barrel uniformity, surface finish, and soundness to verify alignment with customer procurement requirements.

06

Dispatch & Tamil Nadu Supply

Safe stacking in our Manamadurai yard, secure crane loading, and coordinated transport dispatch for direct project site delivery across districts in Tamil Nadu.

Please Note: The production stages outlined above represent standard RCC pipe manufacturing procedures maintained at our Manamadurai operations. Exact batching parameters and project-specific handling are tailored to customer delivery schedules.
